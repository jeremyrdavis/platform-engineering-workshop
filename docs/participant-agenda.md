# Platform Engineering Workshop
## Building Your Internal Developer Platform

**Date:** [Date TBD]
**Time:** 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM (8 hours)

### Welcome

Welcome to this interactive, hands-on workshop designed specifically for technical leaders, architects, and engineers from OpenShift and application development teams. This full-day session will help you understand platform engineering principles, develop concrete goals and action items, and create a roadmap for building or enhancing your internal developer platform.

Throughout the day, you'll participate in a mix of presentations, collaborative discussions, and interactive activities. Expect to be active, engaged, and moving between table work and standing exercises. By the end of the day, you'll leave with actionable deliverables including OKRs, platform requirements, and communication strategies.

---

## Workshop Schedule

### Morning Block (9:00 AM - 12:00 PM)

**9:00 AM - 9:50 AM: Future-State Visioning Exercise**
Kick off the day by imagining your organization's success story with platform engineering. Working in small groups, create an aspirational vision of what your platform team will achieve.

**10:00 AM - 11:15 AM: Foundational Presentations**
Learn the core concepts that underpin successful platform engineering initiatives, including platform team structures, the flywheel effect, and how internal developer platforms transform developer experience.

**11:15 AM - 11:30 AM: Mid-Morning Break**

**11:30 AM - 12:00 PM: Goals and OKRs for Platform Teams**
Discover how to set meaningful objectives and key results that align platform initiatives with business outcomes and enable effective measurement of success.

---

### Midday Break

**12:00 PM - 1:00 PM: Lunch Break**

---

### Afternoon Block Part 1 (1:00 PM - 3:15 PM)

**1:00 PM - 2:00 PM: OKRs and Goals Development Activity**
Apply what you've learned by creating draft objectives, key results, and measurement strategies for your platform team. Collaborate in small groups and share your approaches.

**2:10 PM - 2:40 PM: Red Hat Developer Hub and OpenShift Dev Spaces**
See platform capabilities in action through live demonstrations of Red Hat Developer Hub and OpenShift Dev Spaces, showcasing how these tools enable self-service developer experiences.

**2:50 PM - 3:15 PM: Platform Capabilities and Golden Paths**
Explore self-service capabilities, golden paths, and the key requirements that make platforms valuable to development teams.

---

### Afternoon Block Part 2 (3:15 PM - 5:00 PM)

**3:15 PM - 3:30 PM: Mid-Afternoon Break**

**3:30 PM - 4:30 PM: Platform Requirements Gathering Activity**
Identify current pain points and desired capabilities through an interactive post-it note exercise. Work collaboratively to prioritize requirements and cluster related needs.

**4:40 PM - 4:50 PM: Internal Marketing and Communication Strategies**
Learn proven approaches for communicating platform value, driving adoption, and engaging stakeholders across your organization.

**4:50 PM - 5:00 PM: Closing Synthesis and Next Steps**
Review the day's outputs, connect insights to action items, and establish concrete next steps for your platform engineering journey.
