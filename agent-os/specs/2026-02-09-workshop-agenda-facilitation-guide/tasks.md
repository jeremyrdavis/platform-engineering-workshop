- Prioritize participant engagement and energy management
- Create tangible, actionable outputs participants can implement

- Design for flexibility to adapt to participant needs and pace

### Facilitator Support Focus
- Provide exact scripts and timing to reduce facilitator cognitive load
- Include troubleshooting for common challenges to build facilitator confidence
- Offer discussion prompt banks to support facilitation of interactive sessions
- Supply example outputs to guide facilitators in setting expectations

### Quality Standards
- All timing must be exact and account for transitions
- Materials lists must be comprehensive and organized by session
- Instructions must be detailed enough for first-time facilitators
- Documents must be professional, clear, and export cleanly to PDF
- Content must align with Red Hat's platform engineering approach and materials

### Success Metrics
- Facilitator guide provides sufficient detail for confident delivery
