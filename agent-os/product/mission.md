# Product Mission

## Pitch
The Platform Engineering Workshop for OpenShift is a 1-day interactive experience that helps technical leaders, architects, and engineering teams transform their OpenShift deployment into a high-velocity internal developer platform by providing practical frameworks, collaborative exercises, and actionable strategies for platform team success.

## Users

### Primary Customers
- **Red Hat OpenShift Customers**: Organizations running OpenShift in on-premises datacenters who want to maximize platform adoption and developer productivity
- **Red Hat Sales & Customer Success**: Teams delivering value-added services and enablement to OpenShift customers

### User Personas

**Platform Engineering Leader** (35-50)
- **Role:** Director of Platform Engineering, VP of Engineering, Technical Lead
- **Context:** Responsible for OpenShift platform strategy and team productivity across the organization
- **Pain Points:** Difficulty getting development teams to adopt the platform, lack of clarity on platform team priorities, challenges measuring platform success
- **Goals:** Create a clear platform roadmap aligned with business objectives, improve developer satisfaction, reduce time-to-production for new applications

**Platform Architect** (30-45)
- **Role:** Solutions Architect, Platform Architect, Principal Engineer
- **Context:** Designs and implements OpenShift-based solutions for internal development teams
- **Pain Points:** Balancing standardization with team autonomy, unclear requirements from app dev teams, technical decisions made in isolation
- **Goals:** Design golden paths that developers actually use, create self-service capabilities that reduce platform team toil, establish clear platform standards

**Application Development Team Lead** (28-40)
- **Role:** Engineering Manager, Tech Lead, Senior Developer
- **Context:** Leads teams building applications that deploy to OpenShift
- **Pain Points:** Slow onboarding to the platform, unclear documentation, friction in deployment processes, lack of visibility into platform capabilities
- **Goals:** Deploy applications quickly and safely, understand platform capabilities to leverage them effectively, provide feedback that shapes platform direction

## The Problem

### Platform Teams Build in Isolation Without Developer Input
Platform teams often create capabilities based on assumptions rather than real developer needs, resulting in low adoption, frustrated developers, and duplicated effort. This leads to shadow IT, manual workarounds, and platforms that don't deliver ROI.

**Our Solution:** Interactive workshop activities that bring platform teams and app dev teams together to co-create goals, identify platform requirements, and establish ongoing communication patterns.

### Organizations Lack Frameworks for Platform Success
Teams struggle to measure platform effectiveness, prioritize features, and demonstrate business value. Without clear metrics and goals, platform initiatives lose executive support and funding.

**Our Solution:** Structured exercises to define OKRs, identify value stream alignment opportunities, and establish metrics that connect platform work to business outcomes.

### Platform Adoption Stalls Without Internal Marketing
Even well-designed platforms fail if developers don't know what's available or how to use it. Teams lack strategies for broadcasting new capabilities and celebrating platform wins.

**Our Solution:** Collaborative activities to create internal marketing approaches that keep developers informed and engaged with platform evolution.

## Differentiators

### Hands-On Collaboration, Not Just Lectures
Unlike traditional technical training, this workshop uses interactive exercises with post-it notes, whiteboards, and group discussions to create real artifacts participants can use immediately. Teams leave with OKRs, action items, and marketing strategies they created together.

### OpenShift-Specific Platform Engineering
While generic platform engineering content exists, this workshop customizes proven approaches specifically for OpenShift deployments in customer datacenters, addressing the unique challenges of on-premises environments and Red Hat tooling.

### Bridges Platform and App Dev Teams
Most workshops target either platform teams or developers. This brings both groups together to align on shared goals, creating the cross-functional understanding essential for platform success.

### Practical Framework for Organizational Change
Rather than focusing solely on technical implementation, this workshop addresses the organizational and cultural aspects of platform engineering, including team structures, communication patterns, and change management.

## Key Features

### Core Workshop Components
- **Platform Engineering Fundamentals**: Introduction to platform engineering principles, the flywheel effect, and how platforms create developer velocity
- **Developer Hub Deep Dive**: Exploration of Backstage (Red Hat Developer Hub) as a central portal for self-service platform capabilities
- **Value Stream Alignment**: Framework for identifying how platform work connects to business value and prioritizing based on impact
- **Platform Team Structures**: Models for organizing platform teams, including the enabling team concept and how to balance centralization with autonomy

### Interactive Exercises
- **OKR Development Workshop**: Facilitated activity where participants create objectives and key results for their platform team's annual goals
- **Platform Requirements Gathering**: Collaborative exercise to identify what the platform needs to make development teams productive
- **Internal Marketing Strategy**: Group brainstorming to develop approaches for broadcasting platform updates and driving adoption

### Advanced Topics
- **Golden Paths and Self-Service**: Strategies for creating paved roads that guide developers toward best practices while maintaining flexibility
- **Metrics and Observability**: Frameworks for measuring platform health, developer experience, and business impact
- **Security and Compliance**: Approaches for embedding security and compliance into platform capabilities without creating friction
- **Onboarding Excellence**: Best practices for bringing new applications and teams onto the platform efficiently

### Deliverables
- **Workshop Artifacts**: Post-it note captures, whiteboard photos, and documented outputs from all interactive exercises
- **Action Item List**: Prioritized next steps with owners and timelines
- **OKR Document**: Draft objectives and key results ready for refinement and implementation
- **Marketing Plan**: Initial approach for communicating platform capabilities to development teams
