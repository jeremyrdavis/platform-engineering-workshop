# Tech Stack

This document defines the technical stack for creating and delivering the Platform Engineering Workshop materials.

## Content Creation & Documentation

### Documentation Format
- **Primary Format:** Markdown
- **Purpose:** All workshop content, facilitator guides, participant handouts, and exercise templates
- **Tooling:** Standard Markdown editors, version control via Git

### Presentation Tools
- **Presentation Platform:** Google Slides
- **Purpose:** All workshop presentations (fundamentals, Developer Hub, value streams, team structures, etc.)
- **Export Formats:** PDF for offline access, presenter notes for facilitators

## Workshop Delivery

### Collaboration Tools
- **Physical Materials:** Post-it notes, whiteboards, markers
- **Purpose:** Interactive exercises for OKR development, requirements gathering, and marketing strategy sessions
- **Documentation:** Digital camera or smartphone for capturing whiteboard/post-it outputs

### Discussion Framework
- **Format:** In-person facilitated discussions
- **Timing Tools:** Timer/clock for session management
- **Participant Engagement:** Round-robin discussion, small group breakouts, full group sharing

## Reference Platform

### OpenShift Environment
- **Platform:** Red Hat OpenShift
- **Environment Type:** OpenShift Dev Spaces (for reference and demonstration purposes only)
- **Purpose:** Show real examples of Developer Hub, golden paths, and platform capabilities
- **Note:** No hands-on labs or deployment exercises required

### Developer Hub
- **Tool:** Red Hat Developer Hub (Backstage)
- **Purpose:** Discussion topic and demonstration of platform portal capabilities
- **Usage:** Screenshots, architecture diagrams, and conceptual explanations

## Content Management

### Version Control
- **System:** Git
- **Repository Structure:** Organized by presentation/activity with version tracking for iterations
- **Collaboration:** Enable multiple facilitators and content creators to contribute

### Asset Management
- **Images/Diagrams:** PNG, JPEG for presentation graphics
- **Templates:** Markdown and Google Docs formats for participant exercises
- **Examples:** Real-world anonymized case studies and sample OKRs/metrics

## Distribution & Sharing

### Pre-Workshop Materials
- **Format:** PDF exports of agenda and pre-reading materials
- **Delivery:** Email with calendar invite and logistics

### Post-Workshop Materials
- **Deliverables:** Markdown documents with exercise outputs, action items, OKRs
- **Photos:** Digital captures of whiteboard sessions and post-it note exercises
- **Follow-Up:** Email templates with links to resources and next steps

## Resource Library

### Reference Materials
- **Documentation Links:** Red Hat Developer Hub docs, OpenShift documentation, platform engineering resources
- **Further Reading:** Curated list of books, articles, and case studies
- **Community:** Links to platform engineering communities, Backstage community, Red Hat customer forums

### Templates and Examples
- **OKR Templates:** Sample objectives and key results for platform teams
- **Golden Path Examples:** Template repositories and scaffolding examples
- **Metrics Dashboards:** Example Grafana/Kibana dashboard configs for platform metrics

## Facilitator Tools

### Facilitation Aids
- **Timing Guide:** Detailed schedule with buffer time and break management
- **Question Banks:** Pre-prepared discussion questions and conversation starters
- **Troubleshooting Guide:** Common challenges and facilitation responses

### Training Materials
- **Facilitator Training:** Video or document-based training for new facilitators
- **Practice Sessions:** Dry-run checklist and peer feedback framework
- **Continuous Improvement:** Feedback collection and iteration process

## Platform Engineering Concepts Coverage

### Core Topics
- Platform engineering principles and the flywheel effect
- Value stream alignment and business impact
- Platform team structures and enabling teams
- Developer experience and internal developer platforms

### Technical Topics
- Golden paths and self-service capabilities
- Metrics, observability, and DORA metrics
- Security, compliance, and policy-as-code
- Application and team onboarding processes

### Organizational Topics
- Internal marketing and communication strategies
- OKR development for platform teams
- Cross-functional collaboration patterns
- Change management and adoption strategies
